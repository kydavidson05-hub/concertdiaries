My Concert Experiences

I created The Concert Diaries to share my love for live music and some of my favorite concert memories. I enjoy listening to different types of music and getting to experience my favorite artists in person.

A few of my favorite things about going to concerts is that every show is different, I get to dress up cute, and make fun memories with my friends.

Artists I Have Seen Live

Over the years, I have been lucky enough to attend concerts from many different artists and genres. Some of the artists I have seen include:

  • The Weeknd
  • Kesha
  • The Kid LAROI
  • Empire of the Sun
  • Tate McRae
  • Journey
  • Ariana Grande
  • Def Leppard

My Favorite Part of Live Music

I love that concerts give people the opportunity to connect through music. There is something exciting about singing along with thousands of people who love the same songs as you.

I also enjoy taking pictures and videos at concerts so I can look back at the experience. Every picture reminds me of the fun memories from that night.
